# THE PLAN · Amīratu al-'Ulūm World

*The end result, and the road to it. Kept current; every phase ships live with
side-by-side proof at 90% against the references before the next begins.*

## What this game is, in one breath

A vast night world of Islamic civilization — real as Afghan Ursilat, alive as
Bannerlord, crowned by a fantasy palace of gold domes on a great hill — shaped
by the owner's own hands (the map table draws the land, the editor builds the
places), walked in first person, and harvested for knowledge: manuscripts
hidden in caves, palaces, huge trees and deep seas, collected into books of the
sciences and rewarded; defended, when the time comes, by the sword.

## The laws that govern everything

1. Aniconism: no living beings depicted. Book pages blank. (Enemies may one
   day be the single exception — only by the owner's explicit ruling.)
2. Night only. There is no day mode. Light means torches, lanterns, the moon.
3. The 90% rule covers EVERY SINGLE THING — terrain, trees, palms, rivers,
   flowers, props, interiors, even the moon (sky, moon and its light are
   already judged good) — compared in detail against Afghan Ursilat,
   Bannerlord (look up more reference pictures freely whenever needed), and
   the six fantasy images for the palace. If the comparison fails, the work
   continues. No shipping at "close enough."
4. Huge means huge. When the owner says massive, it is taken literally.
5. The beloved rough ashlar stone is the main design for future buildings;
   the palace wears it a touch whiter. Mudbrick keeps its six approved moods.
6. Plain English everywhere. Show, don't tell: screenshots, not claims.
7. Nothing borrowed from licensed games' files — reproduced by eye and
   measurement from free sources, never extracted.

## The road

### Phase 1 — The world perfected  ← NOW
Purge every last poly-looking asset (old small trees, old bushes, old flower
models). Rebuild flowers as painted petal cards — darker pigment true to the
land, lighting normally like everything else — and sow a superworld of them:
drifts and rivers of bloom. Raise lushness and realistic-tree counts. Bring
hills and mountains to the Bannerlord bar: rolling grass swells, real rock
walls. Proof: same-angle side-by-side crops vs the reference screenshots.

### Phase 2 — The crown: hill, castle, gold domes
Raise the great hill under the town. Build the castle above it in whiter
ashlar: enormous walls, huge towers — fantasy-grade scale, dwarfing the
citadel. The gold domes: smooth high-resolution curves, real ornamental
profiles (swelling onion forms, ribs, tiers, finials), copied at 90% from the
owner's fantasy images. **The images are DELIVERED (six, kept locally at
shots/ref/fantasy_1-6): the amber mega-citadel of stacked domes is the
massing law; the white-gold palace is the whiter-ashlar mood; the majlis
interior is the interiors-and-props law. The old grand hall RETIRES. Every
build gets a finished interior; hundreds of props if needed, all at 90%.
The massing is still shown and approved before the final build.**

### Phase 3 — The 50km circle
The world becomes a circle fifty kilometres across, ending in open sea — an
ORGANIC edge, ruled: the coastline bulges and bends inward, carries hills,
mountains, dunes and river mouths, beautiful and real at 90%. The map table becomes circular at that scale —
one brush pixel ≈ 100–200 m. Streaming keeps memory flat like Minecraft:
only the near world exists; a far ring of simplified terrain carries the
horizon. Performance proven on modest hardware before shipping.

### Phase 4 — The living places
Places that open and reward entering: palace halls, libraries, caves, special
houses, islands, the insides of huge trees. Camps, ruins and watch-places
scattered by the map's stamps and by chance. Doors, stairs and interiors all
walkable and solid — the world invites exploring because exploring pays.

### Phase 5 — The harvest (the game's soul)
The manuscript system: hidden manuscripts, each carrying a hadith, a fā'idah,
a Qur'ān knowledge; each joins a collection by topic; a completed collection
becomes a book and earns its prize. Special items — books, artifacts, tools,
weapons — with glows and a real acquisition moment. Swimming and deep diving:
corals, reefs, great shells holding pearls that serve one of the 'ulūm.

### Phase 6 — The sword
Weapons with real feel (weapon-raised view, no hands shown), aim, recoil,
ammunition. Destruction in honest stages: cracked walls, rubble, falling
chunks. Enemy holds generated across the wilds like Minecraft's outposts;
occasional battles; loot on victory. **Gate: the owner's ruling on enemies
and aniconism decides the form enemies take.**

### Phase 7 — The finish
Ambient sound (wind, water, fire — no music), saving, performance passes,
the first-hour experience. The editor and map table polished as the owner's
permanent tools.

## What the owner provides at each gate

- Phase 2: approval of the shown massing (images already delivered).
- Phase 3: any redraw of the 50km map.
- Phase 6: the enemy/aniconism ruling.
- Always: rebukes with pictures. They are the compass.
