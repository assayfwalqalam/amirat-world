# الرَّوْضَة · Amīratu al-ʿUlūm — The World

An open night world that runs in a browser, on a phone or a computer.

**Play:** https://assayfwalqalam.github.io/amirat-world/

- Drag the right side of the screen to look, the left side to walk (or WASD).
- Space to jump, F to fly, E to open doors.
- Stops drawing when you stop playing, so it costs the device nothing while idle.

## What is in it

The land is generated, not placed: dunes, rocky hills, grassland with flowers,
rivers, lakes and oases continue in every direction as you walk. Inside it stand
a walled town with a great gate, walkable ramparts and stairs, a friday mosque
with its dome and minaret, a palace, a library, houses you can enter, desert
camps around their fires, and a cave lit from within.
