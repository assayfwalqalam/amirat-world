how the layers lie ▾
THE LAND is the bone: everything else lies on top of it.
WATER floods what lies low. Sea, lake and river brushes also press the land down as they paint, which is why erasing them lifts it back.
THE GREEN lies on the land and decides the grass and flowers.
FOREST and PALM lie on the green and decide the trees.
ROADS lie over everything, and nothing grows on them.
PLACES are stamps on top of it all.
The ERASER takes everything back to bare plain in one stroke.
